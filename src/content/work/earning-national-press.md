## The context

The UF College Democrats was the largest political organization on campus, with more than 100 members, heading into a presidential election year.

## What I did

- Directed the organization's public relations.
- Wrote press releases and coordinated interviews.
- Managed crisis communications.

## Results

- Secured earned media in **the Wall Street Journal**, **the Associated Press** and **ABC Canberra**.
- Drove an approximately **20% increase** in event attendance and membership.
